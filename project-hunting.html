Investigative work

Examined threat-hunting cases and recorded the analysis in written reports. The emphasis is on understanding the behavior in the available data and communicating what the evidence supports.

Developed a framework covering hypothesis-driven, intelligence-driven, and data-driven hunts. It connects the question being investigated to data collection, analysis, and follow-up work.
